## What I do

I’m a dispatcher, and I’m all about code. Reviews, builds, tests, that flaky CI run nobody wants to touch: say the word and I’ll get the right agent on it, on your Mac, with your own accounts. Then I tell you how it went.

I don’t write the code myself. I find the best agent for the job, give it a clear brief, watch it work, and make sure nothing leaves your Mac until you say so.

## Hold fn and tell me

Hold the **fn** key, say what you need, let go. I listen on your Mac, only while you hold the key. With my window closed, a small pill on your screen says when I’m listening, thinking and speaking, then goes away. Rather type? Open my window from the menu bar icon. You can also hand me a screenshot or whatever is on your clipboard.

## I start as soon as you've said it

Say the word and the work starts, like Claude Code or Codex: no card to confirm. I tell you which agent, which account, which repository and which branch, under my reply, with **Stop** on the job. Change any of it after: move the job to another tool, account or model, or tell its agent something. Every job gets its own branch, so your working copy stays exactly how you left it. Only when my brain can't answer and I had to guess do I ask first.

## I do it again, on schedule

“Every weekday at 9, review the open pull requests in obi.” I save it as a routine once you confirm it in your inbox, and from then on each run is an ordinary job, with the same checks, scan and review as one you ask for, in the conversation you asked in. If I wasn't running at 9, I catch up once when I'm back; if yesterday's run is still going, I skip today's and say so. Ask me to pause it; resuming, running it now, changing its time or deleting it waits for your OK in the inbox, as making it did. Or do it in Settings › Routines on your Mac and iPhone. Home's Routines shows when each one starts next.

## Then I get out of your way

While agents work, I sit quietly in the menu bar with a tiny counter of what’s working or needs you. Click the Oby icon for **Open Oby**, **Stop all agents** and the rest. When something is yours to decide, I send a notification, and clicking it opens my window on that item.

## You’re the boss

<p align="center">
  <img src="docs/images/app-settings.webp" width="820" alt="Settings: what your Oby may do without asking">
</p>

The rules I live by:

- **I never merge.** Merging, force-pushing and pushing to main stay with you.
- **Nothing goes out that didn't pass.** A pull request opens by itself only once the job's check passed and the secret scan is clean, and it waits for a review before it merges. Every review and comment is shown first and confirmed with Touch ID.
- **I check for secrets** in everything I’m about to send, and I stop if I find one.
- **Every job has limits** on time and cost, and **⌃⌥⌘.** stops all of them at once.
- **I only listen while you hold fn** (or Control-Option), or after fn + Space until you press fn again.
- **No usage data leaves your Mac** unless you turn it on.

## For developers

```
apps/Oby.xcodeproj  the Apple apps: the Mac app now, the iPhone and Apple Watch apps later
apps/mac/           the Mac app (SwiftUI): the window, the menu bar, the character, Settings
Engine/             Oby's engine, shared by the Mac app and the runner: the brain, jobs, agents, accounts, Publish
apps/ios/           the iPhone app (coming)
apps/watch/         the Apple Watch app (coming)
apps/shared/        Swift code the Apple apps share
apps/android/       Android phones and Wear OS (later)
Desktop/            Windows and Linux (later)
character/          Oby the character, shared by every app: oby.html and three.js (see character/README.md)
docs/               the runner's API (docs/runner-api.md) and the pictures on this page
```

Open `apps/Oby.xcodeproj` in Xcode 27 and run the `Oby` scheme on My Mac. Oby needs macOS 26 or later.

**Branches.** Everything starts from `dev`: branch from it and open your pull request into it. When a feature’s tests pass on a real Mac, `dev` is merged into `main`, so `main` always holds a version you can trust. The character’s source lives in `character/`, and `character/package/build.sh` builds the clean copy the app ships.

**Roadmap.** Oby is built feature by feature, and every feature ends with a test you can run yourself. Follow along in the [milestones](https://github.com/deskree-inc/oby/milestones).
